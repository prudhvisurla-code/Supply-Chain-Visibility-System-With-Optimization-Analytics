# Milestone 4 – Warehouse Efficiency & Executive Dashboard

## Project Overview

Milestone 4 focuses on evaluating warehouse efficiency, operational productivity, and overall supply chain performance through an interactive Power BI solution. The dashboard integrates warehouse utilization, throughput, productivity KPIs, forecasting, and executive-level performance indicators to support data-driven business decisions.

## 1. Warehouse Utilization Calculation Methodology

Warehouse utilization measures how effectively the available warehouse capacity is being used.

**Utilization % = (Used Warehouse Capacity / Total Available Capacity) × 100**

The analysis identifies:

- Underutilized warehouse capacity
- High-capacity warehouses
- Potential capacity constraints
- Opportunities for better space allocation

Warehouse utilization is analyzed with operational activity to understand whether capacity is being efficiently used.

## 2. Throughput and Productivity KPI Calculations

Throughput measures the volume of goods processed by a warehouse within a specific period.

**Throughput = Total Units Processed / Time Period**

Key productivity KPIs include:

- **Units Processed:** Total quantity handled by the warehouse.
- **Throughput Rate:** Units processed within a defined period.
- **Order Processing Rate:** Number of orders completed during the operational period.
- **Productivity Rate:** Output generated relative to available operational resources.
- **Warehouse Utilization %:** Percentage of available warehouse capacity being utilized.

These KPIs are used to compare warehouse performance and identify operational bottlenecks.

## 3. Executive Dashboard Design Methodology

The executive dashboard was designed to provide a clear and concise view of warehouse and supply chain performance.

The design methodology includes:

- KPI cards for high-level performance monitoring
- Trend charts for time-based analysis
- Warehouse-level performance comparisons
- Interactive slicers and filters
- Consistent visual hierarchy
- Professional formatting and layout
- Focus on actionable business insights

The dashboard prioritizes important performance indicators while avoiding unnecessary visual complexity.

## 4. Forecasting Implementation Approach

Forecasting is used to identify expected future operational trends and support proactive planning.

The approach includes:

1. Analyzing historical warehouse performance.
2. Identifying trends and recurring patterns.
3. Applying Power BI forecasting capabilities to relevant time-series visuals.
4. Comparing forecasted values with historical performance.
5. Using forecast results to support capacity and resource planning.

Forecasting helps management anticipate future warehouse activity and prepare resources accordingly.

## 5. Dashboard Optimization Techniques

The Power BI dashboard was optimized to improve performance, readability, and user experience.

Key optimization techniques include:

- Using a structured data model
- Creating reusable DAX measures
- Removing unused columns and fields
- Limiting unnecessary visual interactions
- Applying relevant filters and slicers
- Maintaining consistent formatting
- Reducing visual complexity
- Using appropriate visual types for each KPI
- Optimizing page layout and visual placement

These techniques improve dashboard responsiveness while maintaining analytical depth.

## 6. Key Insights

The analysis provides visibility into:

- Warehouse capacity utilization
- Warehouse productivity and efficiency
- Throughput performance
- Underutilized and highly utilized warehouses
- Operational trends over time
- Potential capacity constraints
- Future performance expectations

The dashboard enables management to quickly identify high-performing and underperforming warehouse operations.

## 7. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

- Optimize warehouse space allocation based on utilization levels.
- Investigate consistently underutilized warehouse capacity.
- Monitor highly utilized warehouses to prevent capacity constraints.
- Improve operational processes in low-productivity warehouses.
- Track throughput and productivity KPIs regularly.
- Use forecasting results for capacity and workforce planning.
- Benchmark warehouse performance to identify best practices.
- Conduct regular executive KPI reviews to monitor operational improvements.

## Conclusion

Milestone 4 delivers an executive-level Power BI solution for monitoring warehouse efficiency and operational performance. By integrating warehouse utilization, throughput, productivity KPIs, forecasting, and dashboard optimization techniques, the solution provides actionable insights that support better capacity planning, improved productivity, and data-driven supply chain decisions.
